# Daily Worklog Support

Thanks for using **Daily Worklog**, published by **Son Nguyen Apps**. We welcome bug reports and ideas for making the app more useful.

## Support hours and response target

Support is available Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays. We aim to acknowledge support requests within **48 hours**. This is a target, not a guaranteed resolution time or service-availability SLA.

## Report a bug

Email **nguyenhuynhson140198@gmail.com** with:

- Your Jira site URL and the app environment, if known
- The steps needed to reproduce the problem
- What you expected and what happened
- A screenshot and any relevant error text

Please remove or obscure personal, confidential, or sensitive Jira information from screenshots and messages. Never email passwords, API tokens, or authentication secrets.

## Request a feature

Send your idea to **nguyenhuynhson140198@gmail.com**. A short description of the problem it would solve and how you expect the feature to work is especially helpful. We review suggestions but do not guarantee implementation.
